EMBERFALL DUEL

An original turn-based card battler. Four elements, six links, one duel.

How to play

  • You and Ash Warden start at 20 life. First to 0 loses.
  • Each turn you gain energy and draw a card. Click a card to select it, click again to play it. Keys 1–7 select, Enter plays, E ends the turn.
  • Links: play two different elements in one turn to fire one free combo — all six pairs link (Wildfire, Bloomtide, Ashbloom, Stormsurge, Steamveil, Stormbloom), but only one per turn, so play order picks the link.
  • Chains: play the SAME element twice in one turn and the second card gains +2. Link or chain — every hand is a choice.
  • Read your opponent: Ash Warden telegraphs its next move, so guard when it strikes and strike when it guards.

A complete card game: title, table, both hands, life totals, win and loss — built from scratch, no copied game.
